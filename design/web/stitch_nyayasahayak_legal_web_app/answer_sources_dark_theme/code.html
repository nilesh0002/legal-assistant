What does the law say about personal liberty and being informed of the grounds of arrest?

Short answer

  • check_small Right to life and liberty: No person in India can be deprived of life or personal liberty except by a clear, fair procedure written in law.
  • check_small Right to know why you are arrested: A police officer arresting without a warrant must immediately tell you the full particulars of the offence.

Sources & exact quotes

Constitution of India · Article 21
"No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law."
