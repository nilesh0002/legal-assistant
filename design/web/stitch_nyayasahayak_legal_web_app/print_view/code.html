Question

What does the law say about personal liberty and being informed of the grounds of arrest?

Summary

  • • Right to life and liberty: No person in India can be deprived of life or personal liberty except by a clear, fair procedure written in law.
  • • Right to know why you are arrested: Any police officer arresting a person without a warrant must immediately tell them the full particulars of the offence or grounds for arrest.
  • • Right to bail information: If the offence is bailable, the officer must inform the arrested person of their right to be released on bail.

Official Sources & Exact Quotes

1. Constitution of India — Article 21 (Protection of life and personal liberty)

"No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law."

Source: Legislative Department, Govt. of India · Last verified: 14 Nov 2026

2. Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023 — Section 47(1) (Earlier law: CrPC Section 50)

"Every police officer or other person arresting any person without warrant shall forthwith communicate to him full particulars of the offence for which he is arrested or other grounds for such arrest."

Source: India Code (Act 46 of 2023) · Last verified: 10 Nov 2026
