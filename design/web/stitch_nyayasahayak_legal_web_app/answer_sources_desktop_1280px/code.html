What does the law say about personal liberty and being informed of the grounds of arrest?

Short answer

  • check_small Right to life and liberty: No person in India can be deprived of life or personal liberty except by a clear, fair procedure written in law.
  • check_small Right to know why you are arrested: Any police officer arresting a person without a warrant must immediately tell them the full particulars of the offence or grounds for arrest.
  • check_small Right to bail information: If the offence is bailable, the officer must inform the arrested person of their right to be released on bail.

Protection of life and personal liberty

Person arrested to be informed of grounds of arrest

"Every police officer or other person arresting any person without warrant shall forthwith communicate to him full particulars of the offence for which he is arrested or other grounds for such arrest."
